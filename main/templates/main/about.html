About GameDayPicks

GameDayPicks turns every game on the schedule into a reason to watch, compete, and enjoy game day.

Why We Built It

GameDayPicks started with a simple tradition: getting together with friends and picking the winner of every NFL game each week.

What makes it fun is that you're no longer watching only your favorite team. Every matchup matters. You might find yourself rooting for teams you rarely watched before, following new players, and celebrating or sweating out results alongside your friends.

How It Works

Make your picks for the week's games, compete against other players, and see how your results stack up on the leaderboard. Picks lock when each game kicks off, keeping the competition fair.

Our Goal

We're building GameDayPicks one step at a time, starting with football and working toward making more sports and new ways to compete part of the experience.
