Integrate carefully (Layer 2 only):

1) Add to INSTALLED_APPS:
   rest_framework
   rest_framework_simplejwt.token_blacklist
   mobileapi

2) Middleware (after SecurityMiddleware is fine):
   mobileapi.middleware.MobileApiNoStoreMiddleware

3) REST_FRAMEWORK + SIMPLE_JWT blocks from your ship settings
4) urls.py:
   path("api/v1/", include("mobileapi.urls")),

5) requirements: djangorestframework, djangorestframework-simplejwt[crypto], google-auth

6) On server: pip install → migrate → collectstatic → reload (see STABLE_DEPLOY.md)
